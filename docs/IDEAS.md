## Linkdean architecture

- Linkdean is primarily a self-hosted solution. but I would also like to offer a managed service.
- The managed service would be hosted on a cloud provider (GCP, AWS, etc.), and would be fully managed by the cloud provider.
- The managed service would be a thin/cheap layer and should gradually scale up as the demand grows.
- We'll need a marketing landing page for the managed service, with information about the app, link to docs, github, and a section on membership tiers and pricing information.
