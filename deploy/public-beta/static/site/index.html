A library that stays tidy

File a link once, in as many places as it belongs.

  • Nested folders and tagsOne bookmark can live in several folders.
  • Full-text searchSearch titles, notes and addresses, or jump anywhere with the command palette.
  • DashboardsPin the links you open every day to a home board.
  • Library healthFind duplicates and broken links, and fix them in place.

Private links stay private

Vaults are encrypted folders for the links nobody else should see.

  • Own passphraseEach vault has its own passphrase and recovery key.
  • Encrypted at restArgon2id and AES-GCM. Vault links are never added to the search index.
  • Locks itselfOpens for one session only, and locks again after inactivity.

Share what you choose

Everything starts private. You decide what leaves.

  • Shared foldersInvite people to read or edit a folder or a dashboard.
  • Public listsTurn a folder into a link anyone can read, and revoke it any time.

Bring your bookmarks

Import from your browser or another service, and take everything with you when you leave.

  • ImportBrowser HTML, Pocket CSV, Raindrop CSV and Linkdean JSON.
  • ExportBrowser HTML or a full Linkdean JSON export.

Or run it yourself

The same app, open source, as one Docker image with Postgres and Meilisearch. Sign in with your own OIDC provider.
