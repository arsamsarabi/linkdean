# Public beta and feedback

Linkdean is in public beta at [app.linkdean.app](https://app.linkdean.app). Thank you for trying it.

## What the beta means

- **It is free.** No card, no trial clock. Plans for a paid managed service are still being worked out; see the [roadmap](/docs/roadmap).
- **No uptime promise.** The beta runs on a single server with monitoring and off-site backups that are restored in a test drill, but it can go down. Export your library from **Config options → Import / Export** now and then.
- **Things change.** Features move, and the occasional bug gets through. Releases are listed in the [changelog](/docs/changelog).
- **Your data is yours.** Exports are complete and import into another Linkdean, and the app is source-available, so you can [run your own](/docs/self-hosting/quick-start). How data is handled: [Privacy Policy](https://app.linkdean.app/privacy).

## Feedback

- **Something broke?** [Open a bug report](https://github.com/arsamsarabi/linkdean/issues/new/choose) on GitHub. Say what you did, what you expected and what happened, plus your browser and device. A screenshot helps. Leave out anything private, such as the addresses of your bookmarks.
- **An idea or a question?** Start a thread in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/arsamsarabi/linkdean/discussions).
- **No GitHub account, or something private?** Email [contact@linkdean.app](mailto:contact@linkdean.app).
- **A security problem?** Do not open a public issue. Follow the [security policy](https://github.com/arsamsarabi/linkdean/blob/main/.github/SECURITY.md).

## Most useful to test right now

- Importing your real bookmarks from a browser, Pocket or Raindrop.
- Saving pages with the [browser extension or the bookmarklet](/docs/guides/fast-capture).
- Using it on a phone: saving from the share sheet, filing Unsorted bookmarks.
- Sharing a folder with a friend.
